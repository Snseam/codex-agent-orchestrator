# Code of conduct

We want project discussions to be respectful, useful, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expectations

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; criticize the work rather than the person.
- Give others room to ask questions, make mistakes, and revise their views.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, or private communications without permission.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized behavior, impersonation, and persistent disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for abusive content or accounts. Avoid reposting private or harmful material in a public thread. These reports are handled through GitHub's reporting system; project maintainers also address violations they observe in project spaces.

For software vulnerabilities, use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
